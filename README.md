## Abstract & Research Focus

Oyegoke Oyebode's research centers on multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) coordinated via decentralized ledgers. By leveraging cryptographic verification, autonomous agents maintain state integrity and bias-free adaptation without relying on centralized authority structures.

### Key Contributions
* **Smart Contract Coordination:** Architectures for real-time multi-agent consensus and programmatic operational guardrails.
* **Trustless Adaptation:** Protocols for verifiable reward distribution and tokenized ecosystem optimization in complex environments.
